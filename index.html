How the automation runs

One system watches every resident's membership end-date across all three Club ZAZA amenities and takes over the entire renewal conversation — reminder to payment to confirmation — automatically.

Gym — expiry tracked
Swimming Pool — expiry tracked
Clubhouse — expiry tracked
01
System detects an expiring membership

7 days before any resident's Gym, Pool or Clubhouse access ends, the system flags it automatically — no staff member has to check a spreadsheet or remember a date.

Trigger · Auto
02
A personal reminder goes out on WhatsApp

The resident gets a message with their name, which membership is expiring, and the exact date — sent from the same Club ZAZA WhatsApp number residents already message the front desk on.

03
Resident taps a button — no typing needed

"Renew Now", "View Plans" or "Talk to us" — one tap replies instantly. Most residents renew in under 30 seconds, right from the chat.

04
Plans appear inside the chat

Gym-only, Pool-only, or the All-Access Club ZAZA plan — with pricing — shown as a simple menu. Whichever the resident picks is logged automatically against their unit number.

05
Secure payment link, no app or desk visit

A one-tap payment link is sent instantly. The resident pays from wherever they are — home, office, or out of town.

06
Instant confirmation + digital access card

The moment payment clears, the resident gets a confirmation and a digital membership card on WhatsApp. The front desk's records update in the background — nothing to enter manually.
